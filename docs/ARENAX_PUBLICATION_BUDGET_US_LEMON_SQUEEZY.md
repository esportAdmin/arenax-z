# Arena-X Publication Budget

Market: United States  
Product: retention platform for structured gaming communities  
Primary audience: Discord server admins, Twitch creators, gaming community operators  
Payment provider: Lemon Squeezy, United States  
Date: April 10, 2026

## Executive Summary

Arena-X can be published with a lean but serious monthly operating budget of about **$180 to $300 per month**, excluding paid marketing.

For the first public launch, the recommended planning envelope is:

| Budget Area | Recommended Range |
|---|---:|
| Monthly infrastructure and tools | $180 to $300 / month |
| First 3 months operating budget | $500 to $900 |
| Legal and compliance setup | $1,000 to $3,000 |
| Initial marketing tests | $500 to $1,500 |
| Total recommended first 3 months | $2,300 to $5,300 |
| Lean 12-month cash budget | $12,000 to $15,000 |
| Realistic 12-month cash budget | $18,000 to $25,000 |

The most important business point is that Arena-X should not be positioned as a prediction, fantasy, betting, or money-winning product. The strongest positioning is:

> Arena-X is a retention platform for structured gaming communities.

It helps Discord and Twitch-led communities increase activity, loyalty, return behavior, prestige, and structured participation.

## Product Positioning Assumptions

Arena-X is:

- a community retention platform
- a structured gaming server operating layer
- a Discord-first and Twitch-second product
- a progression, prestige, club, and engagement system
- a tool for admins and operators, not a consumer betting app

Arena-X is not:

- a gambling product
- a wagering product
- a real-money prediction product
- a cash-prize fantasy platform
- a betting odds platform

This distinction matters because it affects:

- legal exposure
- wording
- pricing
- customers
- payment provider review
- marketing channels
- long-term defensibility

## Lemon Squeezy Cost Model

Lemon Squeezy is useful because it can act as a merchant of record. That simplifies taxes, VAT, and sales tax handling compared with managing everything directly.

For planning purposes, use:

```text
5.5% + $0.50 per monthly subscription payment
```

This includes the public ecommerce fee assumption plus a conservative subscription fee assumption.

Additional fees may apply for:

- international cards
- PayPal
- currency conversion
- special payment methods

## Recommended SaaS Pricing

Arena-X should be sold to community operators, not individual players.

| Plan | Target Customer | Recommended Price |
|---|---|---:|
| Starter | Small structured gaming server | $29 / month |
| Growth | Active server or mid-size Twitch creator | $79 / month |
| Pro | Serious community with staff and recurring events | $149 / month |
| Elite | Larger org, creator network, or multi-server community | $299 / month |
| Setup | Optional premium onboarding | $199 to $499 one-time |

Do not price below $29 per month. Below that, payment fees, support time, and onboarding effort will make the business harder to sustain.

## Recommended Launch Strategy

1. Start with a private preview.
2. Validate Discord and Twitch OAuth.
3. Validate profile bootstrap and dashboard.
4. Sell to 10 to 30 structured Discord/Twitch communities manually.
5. Avoid broad paid ads until the activation and retention story is proven.
6. Use Lemon Squeezy subscriptions for simpler tax and merchant-of-record handling.
7. Keep all wording away from betting, wagering, payout, stake, and cash-prize language.

## Key Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Product perceived as betting | Keep wording focused on retention, engagement, live calls, progression, and community prestige |
| OAuth failure hurts trust | Test Discord and Twitch on preview before production |
| Too much free usage | Sell to admins, not individual players |
| Support burden too high | Keep onboarding simple and add a paid setup option |
| Infrastructure overbuild | Start lean with Vercel Pro and Supabase Pro |
| Legal ambiguity | Budget for a non-betting legal review before broad public launch |
